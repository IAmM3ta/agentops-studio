FAQ

Questions worth asking before you send a file

I already paid. How do I send my file?

Follow the steps on the send-your-file page: email the file to robert@agentops.studio with the name on your Square receipt in the subject line. The 48-hour clock starts when the file arrives.

What does the 30 days of upkeep in the Founding Bundle cover?

Pick one during the 30 days: up to two small changes to the file we delivered (about 30 minutes each, such as a new column or formula, a category tweak, or a fix), or one data refresh, where we clean one new batch of up to about 100 rows from the same source. Replies come within 48 hours on business days. The 30 days start when your Rescue is delivered, and unused requests do not roll over. Not included: new builds, extra files or sources, edits inside QuickBooks, Xero, or other live systems, tax, bookkeeping, or legal advice, and calls. Anything bigger gets a quote first. To ask, email robert@agentops.studio with "Upkeep" and the name on your Square receipt in the subject.

How fast is the turnaround?

Spreadsheet Rescue, the Starter package, comes back within 48 hours of receiving the file. That is the turnaround on the Square checkout. Standard, Premium, and a retainer are timed when we confirm the price, before you pay. A person reviews every delivery, so the clock includes that review.

Who can see my files?

Your files are used to do the job you hired us for, and for nothing else. We do not sell them, and we do not reuse them for another client. The cleanup is AI-assisted, so the text of the file may be processed by that tool to do the work. Robert Thomas reviews the result before it is sent back. Working copies are deleted within 14 days of delivery, or sooner if you ask. We can sign your NDA.

What file types do you take?

CSV and Excel (.xlsx and .xls). Invoice or receipt text pasted from email or notes is fine. Scanned images and PDFs without a text layer need a quote first. We would rather quote a blurry stack than guess at a total.

What if something needs a revision?

The report is there so you can check the work. If we misread a figure that was in the file, or mistagged a job or cost type, email us and we will correct that delivery. A new stack of bills is a new job. We will not fill in numbers that were never in the file.

Will missing numbers be filled in?

No. If the file does not say it, the cell stays blank and the report tells you why. Doubtful items, like a tax note that says "maybe 7 percent," or a quantity that could be the unit price or the total, are flagged for you rather than guessed.

What should I take out before I send a file?

Please remove Social Security numbers, full card or bank numbers, passwords, and tax-return packets before sending. Job names, vendor names, dates, and amounts are what the cleanup needs.

Is it 100% accurate? Is this accounting advice?

No cleanup is perfect, and we will not claim that. What you get is a file where every change is labeled and every doubtful item is flagged, so you know what to double-check. Results are for your review before use. They are not accounting, tax, or legal advice.

Do you clean donor lists for nonprofits?

Yes. Send the donor or gift export as CSV or Excel. Possible duplicate donors and households are linked with a reason and never merged. Pledges, unclear amounts, missing names, and unclear dates are left blank and flagged. Each row says whether it is ready for a receipt, and if not, what is missing. We do not write the receipts or change your donor database. You get a clean file to check and import. See the donor sample.

Do you clean rent rolls for property managers?

Yes. Send the rent roll or tenant ledger export as CSV or Excel. Rent is checked against paid for each unit, and short or over payments are flagged. Duplicate units and tenants on two units are pointed out, never merged. Figures are only read from the column that names them, so a deposit is never booked as rent. Ended leases are not rolled to month-to-month unless your file says so. We clean the file; we do not change your property-management software. See the rent-roll sample.

What if my pile is bigger than the package?

Tell us roughly how many files or rows. We will quote it before you pay.
