What the cleanup does

Each currency is totaled on its own

Totals you can trust

  • Open balances, billed amounts, and credits are each summed per currency: USD first, then the others.
  • No exchange rates and no converted grand total. 950.00 EUR stays 950.00 EUR.
  • "$612.50 CAD" is booked as Canadian dollars. A $ sign alone doesn't make a bill USD.
  • Credit memos stay in their own currency and are subtracted only from that currency's total.

Bills, as written

  • European amounts like "1.450,00" are read as 1,450.00, and the row says so.
  • A balance worked out from total minus paid is labeled as computed.
  • A rate or tax that isn't written stays blank. It is not back-filled to make a total add up.
  • Every row has a currency column, so a bill with no currency written can be checked before you rely on it.

What you send

  • Bills, invoices, receipts, and credit memos pasted as text, or a CSV or Excel tracker
  • Any mix of USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, or other currencies with a written currency code or symbol

What you get back

  • A CSV file and an Excel file, one row per bill line, with vendor, number, date, status, currency, amounts, and flags
  • A short report: what's still open per currency, the credits to apply, and anything that needs a person
