Thanks for your order. Now send the file.

Your 48-hour turnaround starts when your file arrives. One email does it: attach the file, answer four short questions, send. You will get a reply confirming we have it.

Email your file to robert@agentops.studio What to send

Checklist

What to send

  1. The file

    Attach an Excel (.xlsx), CSV, or a Google Sheets export (File > Download > Microsoft Excel). For bills and receipts, PDFs with selectable text or pasted email text work. Too big to attach? Put a share link in the email with view access for robert@agentops.studio.

  2. Your name on the receipt

    Put the name on your Square receipt in the subject line so we can match your payment.

  3. Four short answers

    What is in the file. What the finished file should let you do. Any columns, jobs, or categories to use. Anything we should not change. The email button above fills these in for you.

  4. Take out private numbers

    Remove Social Security numbers, full card or bank numbers, passwords, and tax-return packets. Names, vendors, dates, and amounts are what the cleanup needs.

What happens next

From your email to your clean file

  1. We confirm

    A reply confirms we have the file and the time it is due back. If it is bigger than the Starter package, we say so before any work starts.

  2. We clean it

    AI-assisted cleanup, then a person reviews every flagged row before anything is sent back.

  3. You get it back

    Within 48 hours: the cleaned file as Excel and CSV, plus a short report of every change and every item that needs your eye.

  4. One revision

    Reply within 7 days if we misread or mistagged something that was in the file, and we fix it.

The Starter package covers one source file: up to about 200 rows, or up to 25 bills or receipts. It is a data cleanup, not bookkeeping, tax, or legal advice, and we do not log in to or edit QuickBooks, Xero, or other live systems. Working copies are deleted within 14 days of delivery, or sooner if you ask.
