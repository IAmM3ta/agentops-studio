Samples

Five samples, one line on who each is for

Contractor bills sorted by job

Who it's for: Contractors, trade shops, remodelers, and their bookkeepers.

Supplier bills, yard tickets, and a texted rental bill turned into one job-cost file. A job is kept only where the bill writes it; lines with no job are flagged for you to assign.

Checked against a live run on this sample.

Donor list before year-end receipts

Who it's for: Nonprofits sending December receipts from a messy donor export.

Likely duplicate donors and households are linked with a reason, never merged. Pledges and unclear amounts stay blank. Every row says whether it is ready for a receipt.

Checked against a live run on this sample.

See the donor-list sample

Rent roll for property managers

Who it's for: Property managers with a rent roll or tenant ledger nobody fully trusts.

Rent checked against paid for every unit. Duplicate units and tenants are pointed out, never merged. An ended lease is never rolled to month-to-month, and a deposit is never read as rent.

Checked against a live run on this sample.

See the rent-roll sample

Bills in four currencies

Who it's for: Studios and small teams paying suppliers or freelancers abroad.

Bills in USD, CAD, EUR, and GBP in one file, with open, billed, and credit totals kept separate for each currency. Nothing is converted, and EUR is never added to USD.

How a Starter works

What you send, what you get back, in 48 hours

What you send

  • One messy file or pile: a spreadsheet export, pasted bills, a rent roll, or a donor list.
  • A line on what it is for, for example job costing, receipts, or an import.
  • Remove Social Security numbers, full card or bank numbers, and passwords first.

What you get back

  • A clean CSV and an Excel file.
  • A short report of every repair, every computed figure, and every blank or flag a person has to decide.
  • Back within 48 hours of receiving the file, reviewed by Robert Thomas.

Results are for your review before use. We don't log in to or change your accounting, property, or donor software. Not accounting, tax, or legal advice.
