What the cleanup does

Duplicates shown, gifts checked, nothing guessed

Donors and households

  • Likely duplicate donors share a match key and a reason: same email, same name and address, or a common nickname (Bob = Robert, Peggy = Margaret).
  • Rows are never merged or deleted. You decide which record wins in your donor system.
  • Different people at one address share a household key. Jr. and Sr. stay two people.
  • The same amount twice for one donor is flagged as a possible double entry.

Gifts, as written

  • A pledge is not booked as a gift. A hedged or smudged amount, or two amounts on one row, is left blank and flagged.
  • A gift date is kept only when a full day is written. "Sometime in October" stays blank, so the receipt year gets checked.
  • Donor-advised fund, matching-gift, and "on behalf of" rows are marked unclear for hard vs soft credit. A soft credit is never assigned by guesswork.
  • A name is never invented from an email address.

What you send

  • A donor or gift export as CSV or Excel, from a donor database, a payment platform, or a hand-kept sheet
  • Names, emails, mailing addresses, gift amounts and dates, funds or appeals, and any notes column

What you get back

  • A CSV file and an Excel file, one row per source row, with gift, date, fund, credit type, receipt ready (yes or no), receipt blockers, match key, and household key
  • A short report: receipt-ready count, possible duplicates, households, gifts not booked, credits that need a person, and the total of plainly written gifts
