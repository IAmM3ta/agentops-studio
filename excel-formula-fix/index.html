FAQ

Questions

Do I really pay after I see it fixed?

Yes. Send the file or a view-only Google Sheets link. You get screenshots of the fixed sheet working, marked PREVIEW. If they show what you asked for, pay the $150 Starter on Square and the file comes straight back. If not, you owe nothing.

What does the free 20-row fix cover?

One problem in up to 20 rows: a broken formula, a lookup that returns #N/A, a date column that will not sort. You get the corrected formula and a short note on why it broke. Larger jobs get a fixed quote first.

Which formulas and errors do you fix?

#REF!, #N/A, #VALUE!, #DIV/0!, #SPILL!, VLOOKUP and XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, SUMIFS and COUNTIFS, IF chains, text stored as numbers, dates that are really text, ARRAYFORMULA and QUERY in Google Sheets, and broken references after rows or tabs were moved.

How fast is it?

Most single-sheet fixes are back the same day. Every file comes back within 48 hours of arriving.

Is my data safe?

Dummy data is fine for the free fix. Remove Social Security numbers and full card or bank numbers before sending anything. Files are deleted 7 days after delivery. We work on a copy and never log in to your accounts.
